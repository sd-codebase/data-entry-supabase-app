$$ \textbf{Solution} $$ $$ \begin{aligned} 1.\;& \text{Given radius of sphere is} \\ & r = (7.50 \pm 0.85)\,\text{cm}. \end{aligned} $$ $$ \begin{aligned} 2.\;& \text{Volume of sphere is proportional to} \\ & r^3. \end{aligned} $$ $$ \begin{aligned} 3.\;& \text{Hence percentage error in volume is} \\ & \frac{\Delta V}{V}\times100 = 3\frac{\Delta r}{r}\times100. \end{aligned} $$ $$ \begin{aligned} 4.\;& \text{Substituting values,} \\ & x = 3\times\frac{0.85}{7.50}\times100 \\ & = 34. \end{aligned} $$ $$ \begin{aligned} 5.\;& \text{Therefore, percentage error in volume} \\ & \text{is } 34\%. \end{aligned} $$ $$ \boxed{\text{Correct Option: }34} $$
